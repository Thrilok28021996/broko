# Form & Field Registry Matrix

| Form/record | Area | Roles | Trigger | Validation / field mapping notes | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RECO Information Guide | Representation/disclosure | All applicable roles | Before regulated services/assistance as applicable | Record provision/explanation; do not conflate acknowledgement with universal statutory signature requirement | REG-01 |
| Representation agreements (OREA 300/371 and other role equivalents) | Representation | Buyer/Seller/Landlord/Tenant | Based on client/representation model | Verify current member form, jurisdiction and model; alternative form as appropriate | REG-01 |
| Self-represented-party disclosure | Representation | Applicable party interactions | Before applicable assistance | Use RECO form appropriate to representation model | REG-01 |
| Multiple representation disclosure/consent | Representation | Same brokerage transactions | When multiple representation arises | Disclosure and consent event; separate visibility/approval | REG-01 |
| OREA 100 | APS | Buyer/Seller freehold resale | Draft offer | Field mappings verified against licensed supplied form; not universal to every specialty type | REG-06 |
| OREA 101 | Condo APS | Buyer/Seller condominium resale | Draft offer | Verify number/title and version in licensed form library | REG-06 |
| Schedule A / Schedule B / additional schedules (incl. 105 as applicable) | Offer package | Relevant purchase/lease/other | Clause and listing-side attachment intent | Version and priority per executed document, seller/listing-provided docs | REG-06 |
| OREA 320 / 324 as applicable | Cooperation/representation | Buyer/Seller/Landlord/Tenant | Offer/lease cooperation | Role/model/transaction applicability; verify number and content | REG-06 |
| OREA 400 | Agreement to Lease | Landlord/Tenant | Prepare lease offer | Not the Ontario Standard Form of Lease; map conversion/parallel process | REG-06 |
| Ontario Standard Form of Lease | Residential tenancy contract | Landlord/Tenant | Applicable residential tenancy execution | Required for most covered Ontario residential tenancies; exceptions may apply | REG-03 |
| OREA 120 / 420 / agreement-specific amendments | Amendment | All relevant parties | Change executed terms | Never edit original; execute approved amendment; update dependent tasks | REG-06 |
| OREA 121–124 | Condition notices/waiver/release/fulfillment | Relevant parties | Condition resolution or termination | DO NOT auto-assign title to number: source registry verification mandatory | REG-06 |
| OREA 801 | Offer summary | Buyer/Seller | Offer registration/presentation | Broker policy conditional, not universal legal substitution | REG-06 |
| OREA 170 | Advertising consent | Buyer/Seller | Agent opt-in near firm / advertising | Optional general transaction step; marketing-specific consent required where applicable | REG-06 |
| OREA 630/631/634/635 (validate precise mapping) | Identity/funds records | As applicable | FINTRAC triggering event | Regulatory rule ≠ OREA form; verify form/title and duties separately | REG-04 |
| Agent TCS / deal submission | Handoff | All transaction families | Agent final submission | Prefill, agent verification and downstream payload; not entire accounting module | REG-02 |
| Brokerage official trade-record interface | Brokerage handoff | Qualifying trade | Trade records due | Export evidence/structured values to responsible back office; actual official record owned there | REG-02 |
