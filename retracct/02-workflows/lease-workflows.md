# Residential Lease Workflows

Under Ontario law, residential tenancies are strictly governed by the Residential Tenancies Act (RTA) and require the mandatory **Ontario Standard Form of Lease**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Lease Form Enforcement:**  
> OREA Form 400 (Agreement to Lease) operates as an offer-to-lease agreement but does **not** replace the mandatory Ontario Standard Lease. BROKO enforces the generation/provision of the Ontario Standard Lease prior to occupancy.

---

## Landlord Scenarios (L01–L06)
- **L01 · Condo Rental:** Standard condo rules, tenant acknowledgment of condo bylaws.
- **L02 · Freehold House Rental:** Whole home rental, utility transfer terms, lawn/snow maintenance allocations.
- **L03 · Townhouse / Multi-Unit Residential:** Shared property rules, sub-metering terms.
- **L04 · Secondary Suite / Basement Apartment:** Legal suite status verification, fire code compliance declaration.
- **L05 · Student / Room Rental:** Room tenancy restrictions, shared amenities governance.
- **L06 · Lease Renewal / Extension:** Formal lease extension or transition to month-to-month statutory tenancy.

## Tenant Scenarios (T01–T06)
- **T01 · Condo Rental:** Rental application (Form 410), credit report, employment proof, standard lease execution.
- **T02 · House Rental:** Lease offer, key deposit verification, renter's insurance evidence.
- **T03 · Multi-Unit Rental:** Tenancy onboarding and standard lease execution.
- **T04 · Basement / Secondary Suite:** Lease offer and tenant rights confirmation.
- **T05 · Room / Student Lease:** Tenancy execution and guarantor schedules.
- **T06 · Lease Assignment / Sublet:** Assignment consent requests and assignment agreement execution under RTA rules.
