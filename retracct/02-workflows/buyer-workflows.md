# Buyer Transaction Workflows (B01–B07)

This document details the Buyer role workflows. **`B01` Freehold Resale is the primary target for the first engineering POC.**

---

## B01 · Freehold Resale (POC Core)
- **Journey:** Onboard buyer → Determine property/representation/due diligence → Make or receive offer → Manage counteroffers/acceptance → Track deposit & conditions → Assemble verified Transaction Record Sheet (TCS) handoff.
- **Variation Trigger:** Standard residential freehold purchase agreement (OREA Form 100) + Schedule A with financing/inspection conditions.
- **POC Deliverable:** Complete end-to-end journey demonstration with draft creation, e-sign packaging, external signed PDF import, and TCS export.

## B02 · Condominium Resale
- **Journey:** Standard buyer journey augmented by Status Certificate review condition and Condominium Corporation financial review.
- **Variation Trigger:** OREA Form 101; mandatory status certificate review clause and contingency timelines.

## B03 · POTL / Common-Element Condominium
- **Journey:** Freehold parcel with common elements (roads, sewers, parking).
- **Variation Trigger:** OREA Form 111 with Common Elements Assessment review condition.

## B04 · Builder / New Construction
- **Journey:** Pre-construction purchase directly from developer agreement.
- **Variation Trigger:** Tarion warranty schedules, 10-day cooling off period (if condo), builder-specific agreement overrides.

## B05 · Assignment / Power of Sale / Bank-Owned
- **Journey:** High-risk specialty acquisition.
- **Variation Trigger:** Third-party developer consent requirements, "as-is where-is" clauses, strict non-standard condition timelines.

## B06 · Vacant Land / Rural / Farm Residential
- **Journey:** Residential vacant parcel or hobby farm.
- **Variation Trigger:** OREA Form 115, environmental, zoning, septic, and well-potability test conditions.

## B07 · Investment / Tenanted Residential
- **Journey:** Acquisition of rental property with existing tenants.
- **Variation Trigger:** Vacant possession vs tenant assumption, N12 notices, rent roll verification, lease assignments.
