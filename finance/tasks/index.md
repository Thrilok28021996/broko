## Task Workflow Stages

1. **Backlog:** Future fundraising vehicles, cap table modeling, and bank credit facilities.
2. **Ready (`ready`):** Groomed financial modeling inputs, data room documents, and recruitment specs.
3. **In Progress (`in-progress`):** Financial modeling, investor outreach, or automated commission reconciliation.
4. **Review & Gate:** Broker of Record and legal review, trust fund balance verification, and VC term sheet review.
5. **Done:** Executed agreements, approved P&Ls, or closed financing rounds.
