## Task Workflow Stages

1. **Backlog:** Strategic initiatives waiting on sprint allocation or cross-module dependencies.
2. **Ready (`ready`):** Actionable tickets with defined creative briefs, APIs, and targets ready for execution.
3. **In Progress (`in-progress`):** Live campaigns, SEO writing, or Canva template generation underway.
4. **Review & Gate:** Ad performance evaluation vs CPL target (<$25) and RECO advertising compliance review.
5. **Done:** Deployed to production or running live in ad accounts.
